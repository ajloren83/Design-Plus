How to ensure no delays in your renovation works: A guide by DesignPlus

Sep 16, 2024 • 6 min read

Renovation timelines can easily slip when planning, procurement and site coordination are not aligned. At DesignPlus, we bring designers, suppliers and homeowners onto the same page so projects move forward without unnecessary delays.

Plan your scope clearly

Start by listing every area of work, from carpentry to painting and electrical. Clear scopes allow designers and contractors to provide accurate timelines and budgets, preventing late additions that push schedules.

Confirm selections early

Materials such as tiles, laminates and fixtures may have lead times. Confirming selections early lets suppliers reserve stock or propose alternatives in time, avoiding last‑minute changes on site.

Stage work smartly

Good sequencing matters. For example, hacking and masonry should finish before carpentry measurements are taken; ceiling and electrical works should complete before painting. Our platform helps visualise dependencies so teams arrive at the right time.

Track progress transparently

Use progress checklists and weekly updates. With DesignPlus, homeowners get visibility on milestones while designers coordinate with suppliers and site teams to resolve blockers quickly.
