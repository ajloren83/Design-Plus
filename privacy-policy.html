Privacy Policy

Design Plus Interior Pte Ltd ("Design Plus") respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting the personal information that you provide to us. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you visit our website or make a purchase from us.

Personal Information We Collect

When you visit our website, we collect certain information about your device, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and some of the pages you visit. We collect this information to better understand how people use our website and to improve our services. We also collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as your name, email address, and shipping address. We use this information to process your orders, to communicate with you, and to provide you with the best possible service.

How We Use Your Personal Information

We use the personal information that you provide us to:

  • Process your orders and fulfill your requests
  • Communicate with you
  • Provide you with the best possible service
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send you marketing communications, if you have opted in to receive them

Sharing Your Personal Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes without your consent. We may share your personal information with third parties to help us with our business operations, such as processing payments, shipping orders, and providing customer service. We also may share your personal information with law enforcement or other government officials if we are required to do so by law.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we have collected about you. You also have the right to opt out of receiving marketing communications from us. To exercise these rights, please contact us at enquiry@designplus.sg.

Security

We take steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. These steps include, but are not limited to, using secure servers and encryption technologies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make any significant changes, we will notify you by email or through a prominent notice on our website.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at enquiry@designplus.sg.

Additional Information

  • We may use cookies and other tracking technologies to collect and store information about your use of our website. This information may be used to improve our website and services, and to provide you with more relevant content and advertising.
  • You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings. However, please note that some features of our website may not function properly if you do so.
  • We may use third-party advertising companies to serve ads on our website. These companies may use information about your visits to our website and other websites to serve you ads.
  • We may also use social media plugins on our website. These plugins allow you to share content from our website on your social media pages. Please note that when you use these plugins, your social media platform may collect information about your use of our website.
  • We are committed to protecting the privacy of children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that we have collected personal information from a child under the age of 13, please contact us and we will delete that information immediately.
