About Us

Renovation Made Effortless

At DesignPlus, we blend innovative technology with the art of traditional interior design to create an experience that's not only efficient but empowering for every homeowner. With our streamlined platform and a personalised approach, we ensure that your renovation journey is smooth, transparent, and tailored to your unique vision.

"Your home should tell the story of who you are, and be a collection of what you love."
- Nate Berkus

Redefining Interior Design in Singapore

As a leading interior design company in Singapore, DesignPlus goes beyond being just a platform. We are a comprehensive hub where homeowners, designers, contractors, suppliers, and architects come together to collaborate in real time. This integrated approach ensures that every design and renovation project is handled with efficiency, transparency, and creativity, tailored to the unique vision of each client.

Our journey started with a simple idea: to bridge the gap between homeowners and top interior designers in Singapore. Over time, we've expanded into a fully-fledged ecosystem, empowering clients to take control of their design journey while offering access to famous interior designers in Singapore and around the world.

Why DesignPlus?

Simplifying Home Transformations

At DesignPlus, the design process should be as enjoyable as living in your dream home. That’s why we’ve put the control back in your hands. Whether it’s choosing the perfect designer, selecting materials, or setting your own renovation pace, you call the shots—making your vision a reality, your way.

Confidence in Every Detail

We partner exclusively with trusted professionals who share our commitment to quality craftsmanship. Our progressive payment system ensures that you pay as each stage of the project is completed, providing peace of mind while maintaining control throughout the renovation process.

Fair Pricing Without Surprises

No hidden fees, no unexpected markups—just transparent, real-time pricing. By connecting you directly to suppliers, we eliminate middlemen and inflated costs, giving you complete control over your budget. Once you finalize your design, the true cost is laid out upfront, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

A Holistic Approach to Design and Renovation

Whether you're looking for interior designing in Singapore or renovating your dream home, our platform caters to homeowners, professionals, and aspiring designers alike. We provide an inclusive space for everyone involved in the process to connect, collaborate, and grow. With our commitment to sustainability, quality, and innovation, DesignPlus redefines what it means to create beautiful, functional spaces.
