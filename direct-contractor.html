FAQ

Is this really free for homeowners?

Yes. We're funded by our network and partnerships. Your shortlist costs you nothing.

How fast will I get my shortlist?

Most requests are matched within 24–48 hours. Bigger scopes may take a little longer.

What checks do you run on contractors?

We review business registration and licenses, recent site references, and workmanship against our tolerance benchmarks.

Do I have to choose from your list?

No. It's your call. Our job is to surface strong options so you can decide confidently.
