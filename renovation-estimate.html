Home Renovation Estimate in Singapore (2025)

How Much Should You Budget for Your Renovation?

Whether you're planning to renovate your newly purchased BTO, resale HDB, condominium, or landed home in Singapore, the first question is always:

"How much does a renovation actually cost in 2025?"

With rising material costs, changing design trends, and smarter renovation practices, understanding what you're paying for — and what you can save on — is more important than ever.

In this guide, we break down estimated renovation costs by property type, work scope, and lifestyle choices to help you budget realistically.

What's Included in Renovation Costs?

Renovation is typically split into three main categories:

1. Core Renovation Works

  • Hacking and disposal
  • Flooring (vinyl, tiles, parquet)
  • Carpentry (kitchen, wardrobes, feature walls)
  • Electrical wiring and lighting points
  • Plumbing works
  • Painting and wall finishes

2. Furnishing

  • Sofas, beds, dining sets, wardrobes
  • Curtains, blinds, rugs, and decor
  • Optional: Ready-made vs. custom furniture choices

3. Hardware & Appliances

  • Cooker hob and hood
  • Refrigerator, washer, dryer
  • Ceiling fans, lights, water heaters
  • Smart locks and home systems

How Lifestyle Affects Your Renovation Budget

Things to Ask Yourself:

  • Do I cook often? → Invest in a durable kitchen.
  • Do I work from home? → Prioritize ergonomic, well-lit workspaces.
  • Do I entertain frequently? → Consider open-plan designs and stylish living areas.
  • Do I need long-term flexibility? → Use loose furniture instead of full built-ins.

Key Factors That Can Increase Your Renovation Cost

Hacking older resale flats

Rewiring entire units

High-end materials (e.g., quartz, real wood veneer)

Complex carpentry (curved panels, concealed storage)

Designer lighting and imported fittings

Smart Money-Saving Tips for Home Renovation in 2025

Mix Modular Furniture with Essential Built-ins

Focus on key carpentry areas like kitchens and wardrobes. For less critical spaces, use off-the-shelf furniture to reduce costs and increase flexibility.

Prioritize Important Storage Spaces

Instead of overbuilding shelves everywhere, invest in essential storage zones like kitchens, wardrobes, and utility spaces. Avoid unnecessary feature walls or display units that add cost but don't add function.

Use Vinyl Flooring for Budget-Friendly Durability

Vinyl flooring is cost-effective, easy to maintain, and much faster to install compared to tiles or parquet.

Look Out for DesignPlus Collaboration Promotions

Take advantage of special packages and partner deals at DesignPlus. Collaborations with furniture, appliance, and lighting brands often include extra discounts or extended warranties.

Shop Early for Appliances During Warehouse Sales

Major retailers and direct distributors offer significant savings during pre-season or year-end sales. Early purchases allow better price comparisons and reduce last-minute delivery stress.

Consider DesignPlus Renovation Bundles

Bundle renovation services with furniture, appliances, or maintenance plans to enjoy package savings and streamlined project management.

Final Thoughts

Renovation in 2025 is not just about spending big — it's about designing smartly around your life.

Knowing your budget, being clear about your lifestyle needs, and choosing the right mix of custom work and off-the-shelf solutions will help you build a beautiful, practical home without unnecessary costs.
