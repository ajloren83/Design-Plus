A Little About How I Work

Design Process

I would prefer to let homeowners share their ideas and moodboard, if any, with me, in order to understand their lifestyle and what they would like to achieve for their home.

How long would it take to receive the quotation or design proposal?

The design proposal will be ready within 1-2 weeks after First Consultation.

Will the homeowners be liaising with you throughout the entire process of the project?

I will be liaising with the homeowner for all design consultations and quotation-related queries, including managing the project.
